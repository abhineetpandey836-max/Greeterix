Diwali

🪔✨🪔

Festival of Lights
Celebrated in October or November. Homes glow with diyas and rangoli, families share sweets and pray to Goddess Lakshmi. It marks the victory of light over darkness and Lord Rama’s return to Ayodhya.

Holi

🎨💦🎨

Festival of Colours
Celebrated in March. People play with colours, sing and share sweets to welcome spring. It begins with Holika Dahan, the victory of good over evil.

Raksha Bandhan

🎀💛🎀

Bond of Brother and Sister
Celebrated in August. Sisters tie a rakhi on their brothers’ wrists, and brothers promise love, care and protection.

Navratri

🔱🌺🔱

Nine Nights of the Goddess
Nine nights of worship of Goddess Durga, with fasting, garba and dandiya raas in the evenings.

Dussehra

🏹🔥🏹

Victory of Good over Evil
Marks Lord Rama’s victory over Ravana. Effigies of Ravana are burnt on the tenth day after Navratri.

Janmashtami

🦚🪈🦚

Birth of Lord Krishna
Celebrated with fasting, bhajans, midnight prayers and the dahi handi pot-breaking fun.

Durga Puja

🌺🥁🌺

Festival of Goddess Durga
Most popular in Bengal. Grand pandals, dhak drums, dhunuchi dance and sindoor khela over five days.

Ganesh Chaturthi

🌼🙏🌼

Birthday of Lord Ganesha
Ganesha idols are welcomed at home for a few days, offered modaks, and then immersed in water with cheers of Ganpati Bappa Morya.

Lohri

🔥🍿🔥

Winter Harvest Bonfire
Celebrated in mid-January, mainly in Punjab. People dance bhangra around a bonfire and offer peanuts, rewri and popcorn.

Pongal

🍯🌞🌾

Tamil Harvest Festival
A four-day festival in January. Fresh rice is cooked until the pot overflows, to thank the Sun God and the farmers.

Onam

🌼🛶🌼

Harvest Festival of Kerala
Celebrated with pookalam flower carpets, the grand sadya feast, boat races and folk dances.

Chhath Puja

🌅☀️🪔

Worship of the Sun God
A four-day festival celebrated in October or November, mainly in Bihar, Jharkhand and eastern Uttar Pradesh. Devotees fast and offer arghya to the setting and rising sun at rivers and ponds, thanking Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya.

Eid ul-Fitr

🌙⭐🌙

Festival of Breaking the Fast
Marks the end of Ramadan. People offer special prayers, wear new clothes, share sheer khurma and give charity to the needy.

Eid ul-Adha

🕌🌙🕌

Festival of Sacrifice
Remembers Prophet Ibrahim’s devotion to Allah. Meat is shared with family, friends and the poor.

Guru Nanak Jayanti

☬🌟☬

Birth of Guru Nanak Dev Ji
Celebrated with prabhat pheris, kirtan and langar at gurdwaras. It remembers the teachings of honest work, sharing and equality.

Baisakhi

🌾🥁🌾

Sikh New Year and Harvest
Celebrated on 13 or 14 April. It marks the harvest and the founding of the Khalsa in 1699, with bhangra, fairs and gurdwara visits.

Buddha Purnima

☸️🕯️☸️

Birth of Gautama Buddha
Celebrated on the full moon of Vaishakha with meditation, prayers and acts of kindness.

Mahavir Jayanti

🪷🕊️🪷

Birth of Lord Mahavir
A Jain festival of processions and prayers, remembering the teachings of ahimsa (non-violence) and truth.

Christmas

🎄🎁❄️

Birth of Jesus Christ
Celebrated on 25 December with church services, carols, the Christmas tree, gifts and a family feast.

Easter

🐣🌷🐣

Resurrection of Jesus
Celebrated after Good Friday with church services, Easter eggs and family gatherings. It is a symbol of hope and new life.

New Year

🎆🎉🎇

First Day of the Year
Celebrated on 1 January with countdowns, fireworks, parties and fresh resolutions.

Independence Day

🇮🇳🕊️🇮🇳

15 August 1947
The day India became free from British rule. The Prime Minister hoists the flag at the Red Fort and the nation sings with pride.

Republic Day

🎖️🇮🇳🎖️

26 January 1950
The day the Constitution of India came into effect. Celebrated with a grand parade on Kartavya Path in Delhi.
